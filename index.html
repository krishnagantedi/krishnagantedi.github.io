Block Glade: Prism Puzzle

Block Glade Studio

A calm, colourful block puzzle game for Android. Drag prism blocks onto an 8×8 board, clear rows and columns, chain combos, and bring a magical garden back to life.

Game modes

  • Classic: chase your high score on the Google Play Games leaderboard
  • Adventure: 100 levels of treasure harvest
  • Daily Mystery Board: a fresh puzzle every day
  • Zen: no pressure, just relaxing play

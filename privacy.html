Block Glade Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Overview

We do not collect, store or share your personal information. Your game progress stays on your device. The only data collected is what Google gathers to show ads and, if you choose to sign in, to run the Google Play Games leaderboard, both described below.

This policy covers Block Glade: Prism Puzzle ("Block Glade" or "the app"), a puzzle game published by Block Glade Studio ("we"). It applies to the Android app and the web version of the game.

Information we collect

We collect no personal information ourselves. Block Glade has no accounts of its own, no chat and no analytics, and it never sends your gameplay data to us.

The app saves the following only on your device, in its local storage:

  • Game progress: scores, levels, stars, clues, Lumens, garden progress and achievements
  • A game in progress, so you can resume it later
  • Settings, such as music, sound effects, vibration and motion preferences
  • Ad frequency counters (when an ad was last shown), used only to limit how often ads appear

This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it.

Google Play Games (optional)

On Android you can sign in with your Google Play Games profile to appear on the Classic leaderboard. When you do, your Play Games profile name and avatar, your player ID and your best Classic scores are handled by Google Play Games services and shown to other players on the leaderboard. We do not receive your email address or any other account details. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and its game data in the Google Play Games settings on your device or at myaccount.google.com.

Reminders and ratings

If you allow notifications, the app schedules up to one playful reminder a day on your device. These are created locally on your phone, not sent from a server, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android Settings. The app may occasionally show Google Play's own "rate this app" card; any rating or review you leave is handled by Google Play.

Advertising

The free version of Block Glade shows ads served by Google: Google AdMob in the Android app, and Google AdSense (H5 Games Ads) in the web version. We receive only aggregated reports from these services, such as how many ads were shown, and no personal information.

To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android) or cookies (web)
  • Your IP address, used to estimate your general location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system and language
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were viewed or tapped

Ads may be personalised or non-personalised, depending on your choices and where you live. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown.

Rewarded ads, such as extra moves, a free clue or a free Wild Prism, are always optional. They play only when you choose to watch one.

Google's handling of this information is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses cookies in advertising

Permissions

The Android app requests only these permissions:

  • Internet and network state: to load ads
  • Advertising ID: added by Google's ads library to serve ads
  • Vibration: for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's Settings
  • Notifications: for optional daily reminders, which you can turn off at any time

The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or files.

Children's privacy

Block Glade is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age set by law in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Advertising ID (Android): in your device's Settings, under Privacy > Ads or Google > Ads, you can reset or delete your advertising ID. Without it, ads are not personalised.
  • Ad consent: where consent is required, clearing the app's storage in Android Settings shows the consent form again so you can change your choice.
  • Web: you can manage personalised ads at Google My Ad Center and block cookies in your browser settings.
  • Sound and vibration: both can be turned off in the game's Settings.

Data retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove it at any time:

  • In the game, open Settings and tap Reset all progress.
  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings.
  • On the web, clear your browser's site data for the game.

We keep no copy, so we cannot restore lost progress. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policies.

Security

The app does not transmit your game data, so there is no central database of player information to breach. Ads load over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but keeping your data on your own device limits who can reach it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. These rights include those under the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

We hold no personal information about you, so requests about advertising data are best made to Google through the links in the Advertising section. You can still contact us with any privacy question, and we will reply promptly.

We do not sell personal information. Under California law, sharing device data with ad partners for personalised ads may count as "sharing"; you can limit it by deleting your advertising ID or declining personalised ads.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example if we add features or ad partners. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed, and significant changes will also appear in the app's release notes.
